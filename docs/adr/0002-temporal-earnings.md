# Earnings are computed at the rate active when the work happened

Each session's earnings use the rate record with the latest effective date at or before that session's check-in — never simply the current rate. Restating history at today's rate was considered and rejected: a raise would silently rewrite every past week's earnings, and the point of the log is what was actually earned when. The rate shown in Settings is a mirror of the latest-effective record, not a separate source of truth; editing it records a change effective today, and there is at most one record per effective date.
