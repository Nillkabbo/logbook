# Sessions belong to the check-in day — no midnight splitting

A session that crosses midnight (Wednesday 11 PM → Thursday 2 AM) is counted entirely on the day and week of its check-in, so a late night never splits across two days or two weeks. Midnight splitting was considered and rejected: it fragments the single user's mental model of "the day I started working" and complicates every total for no benefit in a personal log. Changing this later would silently reassign historical day and week totals, which is why the decision is recorded here.
