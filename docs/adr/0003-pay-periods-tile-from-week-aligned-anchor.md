# Pay periods tile from a week-aligned anchor

Pay periods are weekly or biweekly: a weekly pay period is simply the configured Week, and a biweekly pay period is two consecutive whole Weeks tiled in 14-day steps from a user-picked anchor that is snapped to the week-start grid — so no Week ever straddles a pay-period boundary and week cards always sum into the period that contains them. Periods bucket sessions by check-in (ADR-0001) and compute earnings per session at its check-in-date rate (ADR-0002); running sessions never count. The period target is the weekly target times the weeks in the period, and an Off week — which suspends only its own week's judgment — never suspends period judgment. Semi-monthly periods were considered and deferred with no migration path promised; the feature defaults to 'none' and every period surface stays hidden until configured.
